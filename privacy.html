Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · Operator: PepeFeels@GitHub

This policy explains how the WoW Forever Armory Bot (the “Bot”) processes data when installed on a Discord server. The operator can be contacted using the details below.

1. Who operates the Bot

Project operator: PepeFeels@GitHub. Privacy contact: 344362929+PepeFeels@users.noreply.github.com.

Important: The contact address supplied is a GitHub users.noreply.github.com address, which may not receive incoming email. Until a working contact channel is confirmed, you can contact the operator through the project's GitHub repository, without posting sensitive personal information publicly.

2. Data processed

To provide the Armory panel, the Bot may process and store:

  • Discord server (guild), channel, user and message identifiers;
  • the member’s Discord display name;
  • character profile paths or URLs and character names, including surnames where available;
  • public character details such as level, race, class, specialization, talents and professions;
  • timestamps, panel state and temporary registration-flow information.

The Bot uses a SQLite database and creates operational backups. It does not require Discord’s privileged Message Content or Members intents. It does not need access to ordinary Discord message content for the described functions.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Data is processed to register characters, display and update Armory panels, manage member entries, recover state after restarts and protect service reliability. For processing subject to the GDPR, the operator relies on legitimate interests in providing and securing a requested community service (Article 6(1)(f)), subject to applicable balancing and user rights. Other legal bases may apply if required by local law.

4. Sources and sharing

Discord provides interaction and account/server information used by the Bot. Character details are fetched from publicly accessible official World of Warcraft Armory pages. Registered character summaries and the associated Discord display name are posted in the server’s selected Armory channel and can be seen by members with access to that channel. Discord processes messages and interactions under its own privacy policy. Public Armory requests are made to the relevant external service.

5. Storage and security

Operational data is stored in the Bot’s SQLite database on its server; backups may contain the same information. The project uses restricted filesystem access and backup procedures. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Retention and deletion

Character records are maintained while needed for the active server panel. Members can remove registered characters through the Bot’s management controls. Removing a displayed character does not necessarily immediately erase historical copies in operational backups. A fixed backup retention period and a complete guild-data erasure procedure have not been publicly verified; contact the operator for a specific request and confirmation. Removing the Bot from a Discord server does not by itself guarantee deletion of its stored database records.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, including the GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict processing of personal data, object to processing, and lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority. To make a request, identify your Discord user ID and server ID where possible, but do not send passwords, tokens or other secrets.

8. International processing

Discord and the public Armory service may process data outside your country. Their respective privacy policies explain their processing and transfer arrangements. The Bot’s hosting location and any additional international transfers should be confirmed with the operator rather than assumed.

9. Children

The Bot is intended for users permitted to use Discord under Discord’s age requirements and applicable law. Do not use the Bot if you are not eligible to use Discord.

10. Changes and contact

This policy may change when the Bot’s functionality or data handling changes. Updates will be published here with a revised effective date.
